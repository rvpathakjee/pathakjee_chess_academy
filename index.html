About Pathakjee Chess Academy

Pathakjee Chess Academy is a small, newly founded chess coaching space based in Vadodara, Gujarat.

It's run personally, one board at a time, without any institutional machinery behind it. The idea is simple: bring chess to anyone in Vadodara who's curious about learning it, at whatever pace suits them.

If that sounds like something you or your child would enjoy, the best next step is to get in touch directly — timings and format can be worked out together.

Founder & Coach

Rajendra Pathakjee

After a long career as a doctor, Rajendra Pathakjee retired and returned to a lifelong love of chess. Pathakjee Chess Academy grew out of that — a way to spend retirement sharing the game with anyone in Vadodara who wants to learn, taken up as a personal pastime rather than a business.
