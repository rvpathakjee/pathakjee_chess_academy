WELCOME

A Gift to the Chess Community

Pathakjee Chess Academy was created with a simple purpose: to share the joy of chess and help people of every age develop concentration, logical thinking, patience and decision-making.

This is a non-profit initiative. There are no fees for learning. The emphasis is on understanding the game, enjoying the process and becoming a better thinker one move at a time.
